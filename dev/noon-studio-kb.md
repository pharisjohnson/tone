# Noon Studio Africa — Knowledge Base

## About
Noon Studio Africa is a Nairobi-based studio helping small and growing East
African businesses win customers online. We build websites, brands, social
media, SEO and AI automation — all in one place, so clients don't need four
different vendors to get the job done. No bloated teams, no account-manager
theatre: clients work directly with the people building their website and
always know what they're paying for and why.

**Mission:** To give East African small businesses the websites, brands and
marketing they need to win customers and grow.

**Vision:** To be the most trusted digital partner for SMEs across East
Africa.

**Why clients choose us:**
- Local insight — deep understanding of Kenyan and East African digital behaviour
- Integrated — strategy → design → build → marketing → AI, in one place
- Transparent — clear scope, clear pricing, clear communication throughout
- Accountable — we measure what matters and report against it

## Services
1. **Web Design & Development** — Custom, responsive websites that turn
   visitors into customers: marketing sites, landing pages, e-commerce
   platforms, and lightweight web apps. Includes strategy & UX, design
   systems, headless & WordPress builds, performance & accessibility.
2. **AI Automation** — Replace busywork with intelligent systems: custom
   GPTs, AI chat agents, content pipelines, lead qualification, CRM
   automations. Includes custom GPTs & agents, workflow automation (n8n /
   Make / Zapier), AI content pipelines, CRM & ops integration.
3. **Digital Marketing** — Strategic campaigns across paid search, display,
   email and funnels, engineered around measurable ROI and clean
   attribution. Includes Google & Meta Ads, email marketing & funnels,
   landing-page CRO, analytics & reporting.
4. **Branding & Graphics** — Logo design, brand identity systems, visual
   guidelines, and marketing collateral. Includes logo & identity, brand
   guidelines, pitch decks, print & packaging.
5. **Social Media Management** — Full-service management: strategy, content
   creation, scheduling, community engagement, performance reporting.
6. **SEO & Content** — On-page and off-page SEO, keyword strategy, technical
   audits, and content production that grows qualified organic traffic.

Most clients combine 2–3 services rather than buying one in isolation.

## Pricing (indicative — every engagement is scoped to the client's goals)
- **Launch — from KES 120,000 (≈ $800), one-off.** A focused starter
  project for new brands or single-page launches. Includes: up to a 5-page
  website, brand starter kit, basic SEO setup, analytics & forms, 30-day
  post-launch support.
- **Growth — from KES 320,000 (≈ $2,100), one-off. Most popular tier.** A
  full digital foundation: site, brand, and the automations that run behind
  it. Includes: 10–15 page custom website, full brand identity system, one
  AI automation (chatbot or workflow), on-page SEO + content brief, 60-day
  optimisation window.
- **Studio Retainer — from KES 180,000/month (≈ $1,200/month), ongoing.**
  An embedded team for marketing, content, AI ops and continuous
  improvement. Includes: dedicated strategist, social + content production,
  paid media management, AI/automation iteration, monthly reporting &
  reviews.

Custom scopes are available — every project starts with a free,
no-obligation discovery call where we scope the work and return a fixed
price before anything starts.
